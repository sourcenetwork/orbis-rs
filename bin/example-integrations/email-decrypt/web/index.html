Email-authorized threshold signing and decryption

Reference app for Orbis's DKIM email-authorization integration. Your own mailbox is the identity ACP grants or revokes — no password, no passkey, no separate enrollment step. This page never verifies anything itself; every ring node independently verifies the DKIM-signed reply you send back before treating it as authorization. Grant your address's identity permission via ACP first — see policies/README.md.
